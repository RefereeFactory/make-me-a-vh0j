How it works

  1. Write freely. Add a title, your text, and pick a colour that fits the mood.
  2. Save. Press Save note — it's instantly stored in Keepsake's permanent cloud, not just on this device.
  3. It remembers. Close the tab, switch phones, come back next year — every note loads straight from the cloud when you open the app.
  4. Find anything. Use the search box to filter by title or text as you type.
  5. Edit or let go. Tap the pencil to edit, or the bin to delete. Deletions are permanent.
